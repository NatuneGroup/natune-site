Welcome to **natune.pages.dev** — the new official home of Natune.

This announcements feed is where we post release notes, known issues and project news. It is served
first-party from this site: the app performs a plain anonymous GET and nothing else — no accounts,
no identifiers, no telemetry.

## What is new

- **15.1.0 is on the way.** The next stable update is being prepared now; watch this feed and the
  [GitHub releases](https://github.com/NatuneGroup/ArchiveTune/releases) page.
- **Natune KMP is in the works.** The Kotlin Multiplatform port brings Natune to
  **Windows, macOS, Linux and iOS**. Follow development in
  [NatuneGroup/ArchiveTune-KMP](https://github.com/NatuneGroup/ArchiveTune-KMP).
- **This site is open.** Found a typo or want to improve a page? The source lives in
  [NatuneGroup/natune-site](https://github.com/NatuneGroup/natune-site).

Thanks for listening. — The Natune Team
