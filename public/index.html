Common questions

Where can I use Natune?add

Natune runs on Android, iOS, Linux, macOS and Windows from a shared Kotlin Multiplatform codebase. The download dialog finds the newest available release for your platform, including nightlies. Android also supports TV; iOS builds require sideloading.

Choose your platform

How do I bring my library with me?add

Export a backup from Settings → Backup before switching devices or builds. Natune supports local backup and Google Drive backup. Keep your original installation and backup until you have checked that your library and settings restored correctly.

Read the latest release news

Which music providers can I use?add

YouTube Music is joined by Tidal, Qobuz, Deezer, Telegram and other configured sources. Choose which providers are enabled and their playback priority. Use your own accounts and subscriptions where required. ArchivePool is optional, and availability and audio quality depend on the source.

Is Natune free, and what about privacy?add

Natune is free software under GPL-3.0: you can inspect, change and share its code. Provider subscriptions are separate. Optional integrations can make their own network requests; review the privacy notice and each provider’s terms before enabling them.

Read Natune’s privacy notice

Does Natune supply the music?add

No. Natune is a player, not a hosting service. It does not upload or distribute music; playback comes from the providers you configure, with your accounts and subscriptions where needed. It does not remove DRM or grant rights to content. You are responsible for following provider terms and applicable law.
